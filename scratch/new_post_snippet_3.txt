## Nervous About Your First Netrani Scuba Dive? Here Is What Helps

Feeling nervous before your first dive is completely normal.

You may wonder whether breathing underwater will feel strange, whether your ears will hurt or whether you will panic once you go below the surface. These are reasonable questions, especially if you have never used scuba equipment before.

The best way to approach your first dive is to avoid rushing.

### Understand the breathing equipment

Scuba equipment allows you to breathe underwater through a regulator connected to an air cylinder. Your instructor should explain how the equipment works and give you an opportunity to become familiar with breathing through the mouthpiece.

Breathe continuously and calmly. Never hold your breath while scuba diving.

### Learn the basic underwater signals

Divers use hand signals because normal conversation is not possible underwater. Your briefing should explain essential signals, including how to communicate that you are okay or need assistance.

Pay attention to these instructions before entering the water.

### Take your time with ear equalisation

As you descend, water pressure changes. You may feel pressure in your ears, so your instructor should explain how to equalise gently.

If you feel pain or cannot equalise, signal your instructor and stop descending. Never force your ears to clear.

### Communicate instead of hiding your concerns

Tell the dive team if you feel uncomfortable, anxious or physically unwell. A good introductory experience should allow time for questions and clear instructions.

You should never feel pressured to continue if you are uncomfortable.
